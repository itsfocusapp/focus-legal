Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Focus collects no data. Not your name, not your usage, not your screen time. Everything the app knows lives on your device and nowhere else.

What stays on your device

  • Your name and age (optional, from onboarding) — stored only on your device, used only to personalize the interface.
  • Your focus sessions, schedules, and blocklist — stored only on your device.
  • Your Screen Time data — this never reaches us at all. Blocking and screen-time charts run on Apple's Screen Time framework (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity). Apple's system renders those numbers inside a sandbox on your device; the app itself cannot read, export, or transmit them. This is enforced by iOS, not by our goodwill.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no third-party SDKs that phone home.
  • No servers of ours — the app makes no network calls to us.
  • No selling, sharing, or processing of personal data, because none is collected.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Notifications

Local notifications (session endings, timer alarms, a one-time trial reminder) are scheduled on your device. Nothing about them leaves it.

Apple Music search (Timer alarms)

If you search Apple Music for a custom alarm sound, that search goes directly to Apple via MusicKit under Apple's own privacy policy. We don't receive or store your searches.

Data deletion

Deleting the app deletes everything, because everything lives inside it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if we add optional cloud sync), the change will be announced in the app before it takes effect.
